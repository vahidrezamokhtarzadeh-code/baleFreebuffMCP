# SignatureBrain Strategic Plan

## 📌 Overview

**SignatureBrain** is a complete PKI (Public Key Infrastructure) software ecosystem providing:
- **CA** (Certificate Authority)
- **RA** (Registration Authority)
- **TSA** (Time Stamp Authority)
- **DSP** (Validation Server / Revocation Distribution Point)
- Additional PKI components and services

**Mission:** Handle current customers effectively, discover potential customers, automate market research, and track business development progress.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Goals

### Goal 1: Customer Management
- Maintain organized database of all current and potential customers
- Track customer lifecycle from prospect to active client
- Monitor license status, renewals, and support tiers
- Ensure timely follow-ups and relationship management

### Goal 2: Market Research Automation
- Identify industries and organizations needing PKI solutions
- Track competitor landscape and market trends
- Generate leads through automated research workflows
- Stay updated on regulatory changes requiring PKI compliance

### Goal 3: Business Development Tracking
- Monitor pipeline stages for potential customers
- Track conversion rates and sales cycles
- Measure customer satisfaction and retention
- Plan outreach campaigns and follow-up schedules

## 📈 Customer Tracking Framework

### Customer Lifecycle Stages
```
[Lead] → [Prospect] → [Qualified] → [Proposal] → [Negotiation] → [Customer] → [Renewal]
```

### Tracking Metrics

#### For Potential Customers
- Lead source (referral, website, event, research)
- Initial contact date
- Last interaction date
- Interest level (Hot / Warm / Cold)
- Next action item
- Estimated deal value

#### For Current Customers
- Contract start date
- License expiration date
- Support tier (Basic / Premium / Enterprise)
- Last support ticket
- Renewal probability
- Upsell opportunities

### Follow-up Schedule
| Customer Type | Frequency | Method |
|--------------|-----------|--------|
| Active/Enterprise | Weekly | Call/Meeting |
| Active/Standard | Bi-weekly | Email |
| At-risk | Weekly | Call |
| New prospect | Within 48h | Email/Call |
| Dormant | Monthly | Email campaign |
